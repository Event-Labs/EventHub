VIETNAM CHILDREN'S LITERATURE CONFERENCE 2035

PUBLISHER CORRESPONDENCE

Dear participating publishers,

We are pleased to confirm the final schedule for the Vietnam Children's Literature Conference 2035.

The conference takes place on 8 and 9 March 2035 at the Hanoi Museum.

Address:
Phạm Hùng, Nam Từ Liêm, Hà Nội.

The public program begins at 09:00.

8 MARCH

09:00 Opening

09:30 Children's Literature Keynote

10:45 Publishing Panel

13:30 Children's Writing Workshop

15:00 Illustration Workshop

16:30 Young Authors Session

17:30 Closing Day One

9 MARCH

09:00 Reading Culture Forum

10:30 School Library Panel

13:30 Digital Children's Books Workshop

15:00 Translation & Publishing Panel

16:30 Book Launch Session

17:15 Awards

17:45 Closing Ceremony

Publisher:

Professional:

Free.

Refunds are accepted until 20 February 2035.

The publisher exhibition area will be open throughout both days.

The exhibition is included in all valid conference passes and does not require a separate ticket.

A proposed children's storytelling night on 9 March was moved to a different venue and is now a separate community program. It is not part of this conference.
