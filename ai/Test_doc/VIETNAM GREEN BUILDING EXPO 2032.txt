VIETNAM GREEN BUILDING EXPO 2032

FAQ

When is the event?

The event takes place from 20 to 22 June 2032.

Where?

Da Nang Exhibition Center, 09 Cách Mạng Tháng Tám, Cẩm Lệ, Da Nang.

What time does it open?

08:30 daily.

What time does it close?

18:00 daily.

What happens on the first day?

08:30 Registration

09:30 Opening Ceremony

10:00 Green Building Keynote

11:30 Sustainable Materials Panel

13:30 Energy Efficiency Workshop

15:30 Smart Building Showcase

17:00 Networking

What happens on the second day?

09:00 Green Architecture Forum

10:30 Solar Technology Workshop

13:30 Building Automation Session

15:30 Green Finance Panel

17:00 Startup Showcase

What happens on the final day?

09:00 Future Buildings Forum

10:30 Certification Workshop

13:30 Innovation Showcase

15:30 Awards

16:30 Closing Ceremony

How much are tickets?

Visitor: 100.000 VNĐ

Professional: 300.000 VNĐ

Full Expo Pass: 500.000 VNĐ

VIP: 900.000 VNĐ

Can tickets be refunded?

Yes. Refund requests are accepted until 10/06/2032.
