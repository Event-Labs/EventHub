VIETNAM CONTEMPORARY ART TRIENNALE 2034

CURATORIAL STATEMENT

The Vietnam Contemporary Art Triennale 2034 explores how artists respond to rapidly changing urban environments, technology and cultural memory.

The main exhibition will be presented at the Museum of Contemporary Art in Ho Chi Minh City from 5 September through 5 October 2034.
