VIETNAM HERITAGE FOOD FESTIVAL 2034

A THREE-DAY CELEBRATION OF VIETNAMESE FOOD CULTURE

The Vietnam Heritage Food Festival 2034 will take place from 8 to 10 June at Gia Dinh Park in Ho Chi Minh City.

Address:
Hoàng Minh Giám, Gò Vấp, TP. Hồ Chí Minh.

Visitors will be able to explore regional food stalls, attend cooking demonstrations, listen to cultural talks and participate in traditional food workshops.

The festival opens each day at 08:00.

On 8 June, the opening ceremony begins at 09:00. A Vietnamese Regional Cuisine Showcase follows at 10:00. At 13:30 there will be a Central Vietnam Cooking Workshop, followed by a Southern Food Heritage Talk at 15:30. The day ends with a cultural performance at 19:00.

On 9 June, visitors can join the Traditional Breakfast Session at 08:30, the Northern Cuisine Workshop at 10:00 and the Sustainable Food Panel at 13:30. A Young Chefs Competition begins at 15:00. The evening includes a folk music performance from 19:00 to 20:30.

The final day starts with a Street Food Photography Walk at 08:30. The International Food Culture Panel begins at 10:30. A Family Cooking Workshop takes place at 13:30. The festival awards are presented at 16:00 and the closing ceremony begins at 17:00.

A Festival Pass costs 150.000 VNĐ. A Workshop Pass costs 250.000 VNĐ. A VIP Pass costs 600.000 VNĐ.

The food market itself is free to enter.

The organizer will refund paid workshop tickets until 25 May 2034.

A midnight street-food event was mentioned in an early promotional poster. Organizers later confirmed that the activity would not take place because the park closes at 21:00. It should therefore not be included in the official schedule.
