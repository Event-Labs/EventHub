HANOI CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY WEEK 2033

What is Hanoi Creative Technology Week?

Hanoi Creative Technology Week 2033 is a five-day public event bringing together designers, developers, artists, educators and technology companies.

When is it?

The event runs from 3 to 7 October 2033.

Where?

Hanoi Creative City,
1 Lương Yên, Hai Bà Trưng, Hà Nội.

When can visitors enter?

The public venue opens at 09:00 and normally closes at 20:00.

What happens on the first day?

The opening ceremony is scheduled for 09:30–10:00. The technology exhibition opens at 10:00. A Creative Coding Workshop takes place from 13:30 to 15:00. At 15:30, visitors can attend the Digital Art Panel. The day concludes with a public installation tour from 18:00 to 19:00.

What happens on the second day?

The morning begins with a UX Design Workshop from 09:30 to 11:00. From 11:15 to 12:15 there is a session about creative entrepreneurship. The afternoon includes an AI Art Workshop from 13:30 to 15:00 and a Motion Design Showcase from 15:30 to 17:00.

What about day three?

The third day focuses on education. A Creative Technology in Schools Forum takes place from 09:00 to 10:30. A student project showcase follows from 11:00 to 12:30. In the afternoon, a Game Design Workshop runs from 13:30 to 15:00.

Day four includes a Startup Demo Session from 10:00 to 11:30, a Creative Industry Investment Panel from 13:30 to 15:00 and an Experimental Media Showcase from 16:00 to 17:30.

The final day begins with a Future of Creativity keynote at 09:30. A community project presentation follows at 11:00. The awards ceremony starts at 15:30 and the closing ceremony is scheduled for 17:00.

Are children free?

Children under six years old can enter the exhibition area for free when accompanied by an adult.

Can I get a refund?

Paid tickets can be refunded before 20 September 2033.

Is there a night party?

A previous social media post mentioned a "Creative Tech Night Party" on 6 October. The organizer has clarified that this is a private partner event and is not part of the official public program.
