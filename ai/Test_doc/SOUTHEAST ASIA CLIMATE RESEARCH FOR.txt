SOUTHEAST ASIA CLIMATE RESEARCH FORUM 2034

CONFERENCE ANNOUNCEMENT

The Southeast Asia Climate Research Forum 2034 will provide a platform for researchers, policymakers, universities and environmental organizations to exchange research on climate adaptation and regional resilience.

The conference will be hosted at the University of Economics Ho Chi Minh City Conference Hall from 20 to 21 March 2034.

Address:
59C Nguyễn Đình Chiểu, District 3, Ho Chi Minh City.

The first day opens with registration from 08:00 to 09:00, followed by the opening ceremony at 09:00. The first keynote, "Climate Risk in Southeast Asia", runs until 10:30.

At 10:45, participants will join a session on coastal adaptation. After lunch, the program continues with an urban resilience workshop from 13:30 to 15:00 and a climate finance panel from 15:15 to 16:30. The first day ends with a researcher networking session from 16:45 to 17:30.

On the second day, the conference begins at 08:30 with a climate data session. A biodiversity and ecosystem panel follows at 10:15. The afternoon program includes a climate modeling workshop from 13:30 to 15:00, a regional policy roundtable from 15:15 to 16:30 and a closing panel from 16:45 to 17:30.

Registration fees are as follows:

Academic:
300.000 VNĐ

Professional:
600.000 VNĐ

Policy Delegate:
500.000 VNĐ

Student:
150.000 VNĐ

The registration fee includes access to conference sessions and digital conference materials.

Refunds are available until 01/03/2034.

An optional Mekong field research trip is planned for 22–24 March. The trip is not included in the conference schedule and requires separate registration.
