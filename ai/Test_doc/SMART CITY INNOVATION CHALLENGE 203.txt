SMART CITY INNOVATION CHALLENGE 2034

COMPETITION BRIEF

The Smart City Innovation Challenge 2034 invites university students, technology startups and civic innovation teams to develop practical solutions for urban problems.

The final event will take place on 18 May 2034 at FPT University Innovation Center, Hoa Lac Hi-Tech Park, Thach That, Hanoi.

Participants must arrive by 08:00 for registration.

The official competition schedule is:

08:00–09:00 Registration

09:00–09:30 Opening Briefing

09:30–11:00 Team Presentations

11:15–12:15 Jury Q&A

12:15–13:30 Lunch

13:30–15:00 Prototype Demonstrations

15:15–16:15 Final Jury Review

16:30–17:00 Awards

17:00–17:30 Closing

Participation is free for registered teams.

Only teams selected during the preliminary round may participate in the final.

An earlier announcement stated that the final would include a 24-hour hackathon beginning on 17 May. This format has been cancelled. The final is now a presentation and prototype demonstration event only.

The organizer will provide basic equipment including projectors, tables and electricity. Teams are responsible for their own laptops and specialized equipment.

A networking dinner may be arranged for sponsors after the closing ceremony. It is not part of the competition schedule.

There is no public admission fee for visitors, but visitor capacity is limited.

Finalist teams must submit their presentation materials before 10 May 2034.
