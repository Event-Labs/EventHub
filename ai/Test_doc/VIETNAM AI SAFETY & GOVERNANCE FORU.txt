VIETNAM AI SAFETY & GOVERNANCE FORUM 2035

BACKGROUND AND EVENT PROGRAM

Artificial intelligence systems are increasingly being deployed across education, healthcare, finance, manufacturing and public services. The Vietnam AI Safety & Governance Forum 2035 is designed to provide a platform for discussing technical safety, regulatory frameworks, evaluation methods and responsible deployment.

The forum will be held from 17 to 18 April 2035 at the University of Information Technology Conference Center.

Address:
Khu phố 6, Linh Trung, Thủ Đức, TP. Hồ Chí Minh.

The venue opens at 08:00.

On 17 April, registration takes place from 08:00 to 09:00. The opening ceremony begins at 09:00 and is followed by the keynote "AI Safety in 2035" from 09:30 to 10:30.

The morning continues with a technical evaluation panel from 10:45 to 12:00.

After lunch, the program includes a workshop on AI red teaming from 13:30 to 15:00, followed by a policy discussion from 15:15 to 16:30. The day ends with a research networking session at 16:45.

On 18 April, the program begins at 09:00 with an AI governance keynote. At 10:30 there is a panel on AI regulation in Southeast Asia. The afternoon includes a model evaluation workshop from 13:30 to 15:00, an industry governance panel from 15:15 to 16:30 and a final recommendations session from 16:45 to 17:30. The closing ceremony takes place from 17:30 to 18:00.

Refunds are accepted until 01 April 2035.

The organizers are also preparing a separate technical training course on 19 April. The course is not part of the two-day forum and requires separate registration.

An earlier proposal mentioned a public AI model demonstration stage. The proposal was rejected because the venue does not have sufficient technical infrastructure. Therefore, this activity should not be extracted as an official session.
