VIETNAM EDUCATION TECHNOLOGY SUMMIT 2032

The Vietnam Education Technology Summit 2032 will bring together education leaders, teachers, technology providers, universities and startups to discuss the future of digital learning.

The event will be held from 16 to 17 May 2032 at the National Convention Center in Hanoi.

Venue address:
1 Phạm Hùng, Nam Từ Liêm, Hà Nội.

The venue will open at 08:00 and the conference will conclude at approximately 18:00 each day.

The first day begins with registration from 08:00 to 09:00, followed by an opening ceremony from 09:00 to 09:30. The keynote session on the future of AI in education will run from 09:30 to 10:30. A panel discussing digital classrooms will take place from 10:45 to 12:00.

After lunch, participants can attend the Education Technology Workshop from 13:30 to 15:00, followed by the Teacher Innovation Forum from 15:15 to 16:30. Networking is scheduled from 16:45 to 17:30.

On the second day, registration is not required. The program starts with an education data forum from 08:30 to 10:00. A session on adaptive learning follows from 10:15 to 11:30. After lunch, the conference continues with an AI curriculum workshop from 13:30 to 15:00 and an education startup showcase from 15:15 to 16:30. The closing panel takes place from 16:45 to 17:30, followed by the closing ceremony from 17:30 to 18:00.

Tickets are priced at 300.000 VNĐ for general attendees, 700.000 VNĐ for professional attendees and 1.200.000 VNĐ for education executives. Students can purchase a student pass for 150.000 VNĐ.

Refund requests are accepted until 05/05/2032.

The organizer has also discussed an optional school visit on 18 May. This visit is not included in the official conference program and has not been confirmed.
